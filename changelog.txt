v2.0.0 (quantumloader)
 * Breaking rewrite (originally .NET 10; see 2.0.1 for VS 2022 TFM)
 * Split into BattleNET.Core, BattleNET.Commands, BattleNET.Cli
 * Async API: ConnectAsync, SendCommandAsync, SendCommandAndWaitAsync
 * IAsyncDisposable / IDisposable with reliable UDP socket cleanup
 * CancellationToken support, PeriodicTimer keep-alive, reconnect backoff
 * Binary Span-based packet build/parse (removed hex/string CRC hacks)
 * Typed commands + players/bans parsers
 * Modern System.CommandLine CLI (one-shot + interactive)
 * Removed obsolete enum members and .NET Framework 4.6.1 client
